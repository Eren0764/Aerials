- Take off, find and shoot down enemy squads with physical bullets and the lead sight.
- Bomb enemy bases (airfields, towers, hangars, flak batteries) with the SM.79 or any plane with a belly rack.
- Land at your base to repair and rearm, then go back out.
- Repeat until every enemy plane is permanently dead.
